# Data card: synthetic scenario data

All files under `data/scenarios/` are synthetic. Each thread or email was generated by `scripts/gen_scenarios.py` from the `script*.md` next to it, with `GENERATE_MODEL = gemini-3.5-flash` (Vertex AI, `global`, temperature 0, through `llm.structured`; responses cached in `.llm_cache/`). The script file is the scenario script (speakers, hidden rule, trap, device IDs, outcome, channel style). Every generated file is `placeholder: true` until a human (Abhishek) has read it. Names are invented Finnish-style names; device and site IDs come from `data/topology.json`; there are no ticket numbers and no real company data.

**Synthetic dates.** Elisa's chat has times only; the adapters date it and Elisa's email 2026-11-01 (DECISIONS item 11). Generated Teams files carry a top-level `"date"`, and the barrier email a `Date:` header, all chosen to fit the story: threads 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-06, barrier email 2026-10-02, key update 2026-11-07. Incidents in `expected.json` are at 2026-11-07 (faults) and 2026-11-08 (dispatch), all `+02:00`.

**Elisa's own files are unmodified.** `data/elisa_examples/teams_chat.json` and `email.txt` are used as-is by `01_core` (via `"inputs"`); nothing was generated from or written into them.
